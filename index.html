Fangcan Chi · UX/UI design and front end

I design and build interfaces for people with slow connections and small screens.

I study Electrical and Computer Engineering at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. These projects take one problem, knowing which schools have usable internet, and design for the people around it: the officials who read the dashboard and the school staff who set up the test device.

About

I am Fangcan Chi, a third-year undergraduate in Electrical and Computer Engineering in the dual-degree programme of Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

I like interfaces that answer a question in one sentence, keep working when the network does not, and can be used without reading every word. I start from the person and the question they need answered, sketch, then build in plain web code so the result stays small and fast. All sample data on this site is generated for a fictional region, and research that has not happened yet is written as a plan, not as results.
