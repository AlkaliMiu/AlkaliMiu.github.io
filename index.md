# Fangcan Chi

Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I design and build interfaces for people with slow connections and small screens.

## Projects

### [Signal Setup](projects/signal-setup/)

A mobile flow that lets school staff set up a speed-test box, designed for low literacy, low bandwidth and cheap Android phones: hypothetical personas, journey, sketches, screens, a clickable prototype and a usability-test plan. [Read the case study](projects/signal-setup/case-study.html).

### [Classroom Signal](projects/classroom-signal/)

A bilingual, low-bandwidth dashboard showing which schools in a region have usable internet: district map, school list and per-school speed history, in one 59 KB page. [Read the case study](projects/classroom-signal/case-study.html).
